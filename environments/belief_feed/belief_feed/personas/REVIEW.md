# Persona review (CLAUDE.md section 3.4, rule 6)

The same checklist is applied to every persona. Mechanical items are enforced by
`tests/test_personas.py` and re-run on every change; judgement items were reviewed by
the authoring agent (Claude) on 2026-10-19. **A human review by Manas is still
pending**: sign off at the bottom, or open an issue against a specific persona.

## Checklist

| # | Item | How it is checked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Length matched within ±10% of the library mean, 150–250 words, at every intensity | test (`test_lengths_matched_within_ten_percent`) |
| 2 | Same intensity wording for every persona at each level | test: one shared `INTENSITY_SENTENCE` per level is appended verbatim |
| 3 | Same skeleton: identity, "What matters most to me is", "I want", "I judge a policy by … and by who pays its costs", "I am proud of", "I am wary of" | test (`test_same_skeleton_and_intensity_sentence`) |
| 4 | No slurs, insults or dehumanising words | test against a banned-word list (`test_no_banned_language`) |
| 5 | No real parties, countries or people | test plus the world name blocklist |
| 6 | Mirror persona = exact negation of the axis vector | test (`test_mirrors_are_negations`) |
| 7 | No straw-manning: values stated positively, as the best case adherents would make | judgement |
| 8 | "Wary of" targets arrangements or ideas, never groups of people | judgement |
| 9 | Values in the adherents' own vocabulary (e.g. "stewardship", "liberty", "solidarity", "continuity", "public order") | judgement |
| 10 | Value items: two agree-keyed and one reverse-keyed, phrased in the same register for every persona | judgement + structure fixed in code |

## Known limitation to review

Exact negation of an axis vector does not always produce the persona one would write by
hand. For example, the law-and-order traditionalist leans slightly state-led on economics
(-0.2) because its mirror, the civil libertarian, leans slightly market-led (+0.2). The
texts describe each persona's own core values and do not mention these minor components.
